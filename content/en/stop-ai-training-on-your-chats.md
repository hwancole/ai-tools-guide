Most free and paid personal AI chatbots can use your conversations to improve their models unless you say no. Each one has a setting to opt out, but the names are different, they're tucked away in different menus, and they don't all work the same way.

> **Quick answer (as of November 2026):**
> - **ChatGPT:** Profile icon > **Settings > Data Controls** > turn off **Improve the model for everyone**.
> - **Claude:** Your name > **Settings > Privacy** > turn off **Help improve our AI models**.
> - **Gemini:** **Settings & help > Activity** > click **On** > **Turn off** (this is the **Keep Activity** setting).
> - **Microsoft Copilot (new app):** Microsoft says prompts, responses and files in the updated Copilot app **aren't used to train its foundation models**, so there is no training switch to flip.
>
> For a one-off sensitive question, use the private mode instead: **Temporary Chat** in ChatGPT and Gemini, **Incognito** in Claude.

## The settings at a glance

| Tool | Setting name | Where it is | What turning it off does |
|---|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT | Improve the model for everyone | Settings > Data Controls | New chats aren't used for training; your history stays |
| Claude | Help improve our AI models | Settings > Privacy | New and past chats aren't used for future training; your history stays |
| Gemini | Keep Activity | Settings & help > Activity | Future chats aren't saved to your activity or used for training; kept 72 hours |
| Copilot (new app) | No training toggle | n/a | Microsoft says chats aren't used to train foundation models |

All of this applies to **personal accounts**. Work and school accounts usually follow different rules (see the section near the end).

## ChatGPT: turn off "Improve the model for everyone"

According to OpenAI's Data Controls FAQ (as of November 2026), the old "Chat history & model training" switch was split into two features: a training toggle and Temporary Chats.

**On the web**

1. Click your **profile icon** at the bottom left.
2. Go to **Settings > Data Controls**.
3. Turn off **Improve the model for everyone**.

**On the app**

- **iPhone/iPad:** tap the three dots at the top right > **Settings > Data Controls** > turn off **Improve the model for everyone**.
- **Android:** tap the three lines at the top left > **Settings > Data Controls** > turn off the same toggle.

Things to know:

- The setting is tied to your **account**, so changing it once applies on every device.
- Your past conversations **stay in your history**. They just won't be used for training.
- It works even when you're logged out: click the **?** icon at the bottom right > **Settings > Data Controls**.
- OpenAI also offers a privacy portal where you can submit a "do not train on my content" request.

**Temporary Chat:** start one from the model menu at the top of the chat. Temporary Chats don't appear in your history, don't use or create memories, and aren't used to train models. OpenAI says it may keep a copy for up to **30 days** for safety reasons before deleting it.

## Claude: turn off "Help improve our AI models"

Anthropic's privacy center (updated August 2026) describes the setting for the Free, Pro and Max plans, including Claude Code used with those accounts.

**On a computer or the web**

1. Click your **name** in the sidebar and open **Settings** (or go straight to claude.ai/settings/data-privacy-controls).
2. Open **Privacy**.
3. Turn off **Help improve our AI models**.

**On the app:** tap your name > **Settings > Privacy** > turn off the same toggle.

What changes:

- With the setting **off**, Anthropic says it won't use your previous or new chats and coding sessions for future model training.
- With it **on**, chats may be kept in de-identified form for up to **5 years** in training pipelines.
- Data that was already used in a training run that has started, or in a finished model, can't be pulled back out.
- Chats flagged by safety systems may still be used to improve Anthropic's internal trust and safety models.
- If you give a thumbs up or down, the whole conversation is stored with that feedback for up to 5 years and may be used for training. Skip the thumbs if that matters to you.

**Incognito chats** are not used to improve Claude, even if the training setting is on. Deleted chats disappear from your history right away and are removed from Anthropic's back-end systems within 30 days.

## Gemini: turn off Keep Activity

Gemini's setting is called **Keep Activity**, and Google says it controls "whether your data is used to improve Google AI." It's on by default for users 18 and over (as of November 2026, per the Gemini Apps Privacy Hub).

**Steps**

1. In Gemini (web or app), open **Settings & help > Activity**. You can also go to myactivity.google.com/product/gemini.
2. Near the top, click **On**.
3. Choose **Turn off**, or **Turn off and delete activity** if you also want to clear past chats.

What happens next:

- Your future chats won't appear in your activity and won't be used to train Google's AI models, unless you send feedback.
- Google still keeps them for **72 hours** so Gemini can respond, process feedback and protect against abuse.
- Some features stop working. Google's help page says only a short list of apps (Device assistance, Gemini Notebook, Messages, Phone and WhatsApp) stays available, and some Connected Apps become unavailable.

**About human reviewers:** Google says a subset of chats are reviewed by people, including trained service providers. Reviewed chats are disconnected from your account and kept for up to **3 years**, even if you later delete your activity. That's the main reason Google advises against typing confidential information into Gemini.

**A middle ground:** if you want to keep your history, leave Keep Activity on and set **auto-delete** to 3 months (the default is 18 months). Go to **Settings & help > Activity** and pick **Choose an auto-delete option**. Or use **Temporary chats**, which Google says aren't used to train its models and are kept for 72 hours.

## Microsoft Copilot: what the new app says

Microsoft released an updated Copilot app for web, Windows, Mac and mobile on August 18, 2026. Its privacy page says that **prompts, responses and file contents aren't used to train foundation models**. Optional feedback you send may be used to improve Copilot, but not to train those models. Because of that, the new app's privacy controls cover memory, chat history, web search and ad personalization instead of training.

Useful switches in the new app (**Settings > Personalization**):

- **Saved memories:** turn off, or use **Manage > Delete all memories**.
- **Allow ads personalization:** turn off if you don't want personalized ads.

To delete chats, open the chat in the **Chats** list > **More (...)** > **Delete**. To delete everything, sign in at account.microsoft.com/privacy and find **Copilot > Your Copilot app activity history > Delete all activity history**.

**Still on the older app?** Microsoft's older help page lists **Training on conversation activity** and **Training on voice conversations** under your profile > **Privacy**. Microsoft asks users to sign in to the updated app by **December 31, 2026** to keep eligible chats; unmigrated content will be deleted after that date.

## Work and school accounts

If you use AI through your employer or school, training settings are usually decided by the organization, not by you:

- **ChatGPT:** OpenAI says it doesn't use content from business offerings such as ChatGPT Team, ChatGPT Enterprise and its API platform to train models.
- **Claude:** the consumer setting above doesn't cover Anthropic's commercial products (Claude for Work and the API), which have separate terms.
- **Gemini:** with a work or school Google account, your administrator controls activity settings and different data terms may apply.
- **Copilot:** Microsoft 365 Copilot used with a work or school (Entra ID) account isn't covered by the consumer settings.

When in doubt, ask your IT team before pasting company data into any chatbot.

## What turning off training doesn't do

- **It doesn't make chats invisible.** Companies still process your messages to answer them and may keep them for a period for safety and legal reasons.
- **It isn't retroactive everywhere.** ChatGPT and Gemini describe it as applying to future chats. Data already used in a model stays there.
- **It doesn't delete your history.** Delete chats separately if you want them gone.
- **It doesn't cover feedback.** Thumbs up/down and "send feedback" can share the conversation even with training off.

## Simple habits that protect you more than any toggle

- Don't paste passwords, ID numbers, bank details or health records into a chatbot.
- Remove names and identifying details from documents before uploading them. A prompt like the one below helps:

> Rewrite the text below, replacing every person's name, company name, address, phone number and email with placeholders like [NAME 1] and [COMPANY]. Don't change anything else. Text: [paste]

- Use Temporary or Incognito chats for one-off sensitive questions.
- Check your settings again after big app updates. Names and menus change often, as the Copilot redesign shows.

## FAQ

**Does ChatGPT train on my conversations by default?**
For personal accounts, OpenAI says it may use your content to train its models unless you opt out with **Improve the model for everyone** or its privacy portal (as of November 2026). Business plans such as ChatGPT Team and Enterprise aren't used for training.

**If I turn off training in ChatGPT, do I lose my chat history?**
No. OpenAI says conversations still appear in your history; they just aren't used for training.

**Is Claude's Incognito mode private?**
Anthropic says Incognito chats aren't used to improve Claude, even when the training setting is on. They still go through Anthropic's systems to generate answers.

**Why does Gemini keep my chats for 72 hours when Keep Activity is off?**
Google says it needs them briefly to respond to you, process feedback and protect users and the public. They aren't used for training in that time unless you send feedback.

**Can I stop humans from reading my Gemini chats?**
Turning off Keep Activity stops future chats from being reviewed to improve Google services, according to Google. Chats already reviewed are kept for up to 3 years.

**Which AI chatbot is the most private?**
It depends on your settings more than the brand. With training off and private chats used for sensitive topics, ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini all keep your conversations out of model training. Copilot's new app says it doesn't train foundation models on chats at all. None of them is a safe place for passwords or highly sensitive records.

**Do these settings apply to AI features in Gmail, Word or Excel?**
Not necessarily. Built-in AI in Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 has its own privacy terms, especially on work accounts.
