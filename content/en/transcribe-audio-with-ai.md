You no longer need to type out a recording by hand. Several tools you may already have can turn speech into text in minutes, and an AI chatbot can then clean the transcript up, summarize it or pull out the action items. The trick is picking the right tool for the length of the recording and where it lives.

> **Quick answer:** If you have a **Microsoft 365** subscription, open Word and use **Home > Dictate > Transcribe > Upload audio** (up to 300 minutes of uploads per month, as of November 2026). For a short clip with no subscription, upload it to **Gemini** (free plan: up to 10 minutes of audio). On a phone, the built-in recorder may already transcribe for you: **Voice Memos** on recent iPhones and **Recorder** on Google Pixel phones. For regular meetings or lectures, **Otter**'s free plan gives 300 minutes a month, with a 30-minute limit per conversation. Then paste the transcript into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini to tidy it up.

| Long recording, you pay for Google AI Pro or Ultra | **Gemini** | Total upload length of up to 3 hours |

| Meetings on a Mac, paid ChatGPT plan | **ChatGPT record mode** | Records in the macOS app and gives you notes and a summary |

## How to transcribe an audio file in Word

You need Word for Microsoft 365 (a paid subscription); the feature isn't in one-time-purchase versions of Word.

1. Open a new document in Word.
2. Go to **Home > Dictate > Transcribe**.
3. In the Transcribe pane, choose **Upload audio** and pick your .mp3, .m4a, .wav or .mp4 file.
4. Keep the pane open while it works. Processing can take up to about as long as the recording itself.
5. Rename speakers: hover over a section, select **Edit transcript section**, and change "Speaker 1" to a real name for every section at once.
6. Choose **Add all to document** to put the full transcript into your document, or add sections one by one.

You can also choose **Start recording** in the same pane to record a meeting or class live. If you're capturing a video call on your computer, don't use a headset, so the microphone picks up the other side too.

## How to transcribe a recording with Gemini (free)

1. Go to **gemini.google.com** or open the Gemini app and sign in.
2. Click the **+** button and upload your audio file.
3. Use the transcript prompt below. On the free plan, keep the audio to 10 minutes or less; split longer files first.
4. Read the result against the recording, especially names, numbers and technical words.

Gemini is a chatbot rather than a dedicated transcription service, so it's worth telling it exactly what you want: word-for-word or cleaned up, with or without timestamps.

## How to transcribe on your phone

**iPhone (Voice Memos).** On supported iPhones, Voice Memos creates a transcript of your recordings. Open a recording and look for the transcript button to read the text, search it and copy it into another app. Apple notes that audio transcription isn't available in every country or region, so if you don't see it, that's the likely reason.

**Google Pixel (Recorder).** Recorder transcribes in real time as you record. Pixel 6 and later support English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Mandarin Chinese and Hindi in real time, and you can choose **More > Transcribe again** to re-transcribe a recording in roughly 40 languages. On Pixel 8 and later, tap **Summarize** to get a summary of recordings with 5 minutes to 1 hour of speech.

**Other Android phones.** Google's **Live Transcribe** app shows live captions of speech around you. It's built for following a conversation in real time rather than producing a document, but it keeps a transcription history for three days that you can copy from. It comes installed on Pixel and select other Android phones and is available on Google Play.

## Transcribing meetings

- **Otter** can join Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams calls and write the transcript as the meeting happens. On the free plan, each conversation stops being transcribed after 30 minutes.
- **ChatGPT record mode** is available in the ChatGPT app for macOS on paid plans (Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu, according to OpenAI's help center). It records a meeting or voice note and turns it into notes you can edit in the chat.
- **Your meeting app** may already include it. Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams all offer built-in transcripts on certain paid plans. Ask whoever manages your work account before installing another tool.

Always tell people when a meeting is being recorded or transcribed. In many places it's required by law, and it's simply good manners everywhere.

## Copy-paste prompts

**Clean transcript from an uploaded file (Gemini)**

> Transcribe the attached audio word for word. Start a new paragraph whenever the speaker changes and label speakers as Speaker 1, Speaker 2, and so on. Add a timestamp in [mm:ss] at the start of each paragraph. Write [inaudible] where you can't make out the words. Don't summarize or leave anything out.

**Tidy up a raw transcript (any chatbot)**

> Below is an automatic transcript of a [meeting / lecture / interview]. Fix punctuation and obvious speech-recognition errors, remove filler words like "um" and "you know", and split it into paragraphs. Don't change the meaning or add anything. List every place where you weren't sure what was said, with the original text.

**Meeting notes and action items**

> From this meeting transcript, write: 1) a five-sentence summary, 2) the decisions made, 3) a table of action items with owner and deadline if mentioned. Only use what's in the transcript. If an owner or deadline wasn't stated, write "not stated".

**Lecture into study notes**

> Turn this lecture transcript into study notes: headings for each topic, key terms in bold with a one-line definition, and 10 practice questions at the end with answers. Keep the lecturer's examples.

**Interview quotes**

> From this interview transcript, pull the 8 most quotable sentences, exactly as spoken, with the timestamp of each. Don't rephrase them.

Long transcripts can be too big to paste at once. If that happens, upload the transcript as a text or Word file, or paste it in parts and ask for the summary at the end.

## Tips for a more accurate transcript

- **Record close to the speaker.** A phone in the middle of a big table catches more room noise than voices. A cheap clip-on microphone helps a lot for interviews.
- **Cut background noise.** Music, air conditioning and cafés are the most common reasons for mistakes.
- **One person at a time.** People talking over each other confuses speaker labels in every tool.
- **Give the AI a word list.** Before cleaning up a transcript, tell the chatbot the names, brands and technical terms that appear, so it spells them correctly.
- **Check numbers and names yourself.** These are the words speech recognition gets wrong most often, and they're usually the ones that matter.

## Privacy

Uploading a recording sends other people's voices and words to the company that runs the tool. Get permission before you record, avoid uploading confidential work meetings, medical or legal conversations unless your employer has approved the tool, and check each tool's data settings. Delete recordings you no longer need.

## FAQ

**What is the best free way to transcribe audio to text?**
It depends on where the audio is. For a short file, Gemini's free plan handles up to 10 minutes of audio. For recordings on your phone, use the built-in Voice Memos (iPhone) or Recorder (Pixel) app. For regular meetings, Otter's free plan offers 300 minutes a month, as of November 2026.

**Can ChatGPT transcribe audio?**
ChatGPT's record mode in the macOS app records and summarizes meetings on paid plans. For transcribing an existing file without a subscription, Word (if you have Microsoft 365) or Gemini are more straightforward options. Any chatbot, including ChatGPT and Claude, is useful for cleaning up and summarizing a transcript once you have one.

**How do I transcribe a recording longer than an hour?**
Word's Transcribe accepts uploads up to your 300 monthly minutes on Microsoft 365, and Gemini allows up to 3 hours with Google AI Pro or Ultra. On free tools, split the file into shorter parts and transcribe each one.

**How accurate is AI transcription?**
With clear audio and one speaker at a time, it's usually good enough to work from. Accuracy drops with background noise, strong accents, crosstalk, and specialist vocabulary, so always check names, numbers and quotes against the recording.

**Can AI transcribe a YouTube video or podcast?**
If you have the audio or video file and the right to use it, you can upload it to the tools above. Many YouTube videos also have a built-in transcript: click **...more** under the video and then **Show transcript**.

**Can AI transcribe audio in other languages?**
Yes. Pixel Recorder can re-transcribe in about 40 languages, and chatbots like Gemini can transcribe and then translate in one step. Ask for the transcript in the original language first, then a separate translation, so you can check both.
